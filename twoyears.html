Two Years


Third day of the first,
Thought he had passed the worst.
Twelve hours a day,
Thought about what he had last May.
He gave it his all, every single day,
Something made him smile, so he figured he'd stay.
But soon his smile faded,
The more they were raided.
Drove him mad by July,
Passed time he could not buy.

Third day of the second,
Could be worse, he reckoned.
Could've played it safe,
Avoided the ankle chafe.
Time revealed truth in the Forgotten's song,
Clouded by naivety, defended them all along.
There he stayed to prove a point,
Complacency by those they anoint.
Watching over the battle field,
The ones who preach never carry the sword or shield.

Third day of the third,
A visit from a special bird.
Did any of it really matter,
What's with all the chatter?
Wish he didn't care,
Should've saved his hair.
Set up from the start,
That's why the town always fell apart.
Last glance back, he stands above the prow.
Wish he knew then, what he knows now.

The End
